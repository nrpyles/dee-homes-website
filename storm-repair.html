Why Dee Homes

One Contractor for the Whole Claim

DFW sits in the heart of "Hail Alley." When a storm rolls through Plano, McKinney, Garland, or Fort Worth, most homeowners get bounced between a roofer, a gutter company, a siding crew, and an adjuster who all point at each other. Dee Homes is one accountable builder for the entire exterior — roof, gutters, siding, soffit and fascia — and we speak the insurance company's language.

We document the damage, meet your adjuster on site, and restore your home to pre-storm condition (or better). In most approved claims, you pay only your deductible.

How It Works

Your Claim, Step by Step

Free Inspection

We inspect your roof and exterior for hail and wind damage and give you an honest assessment — no claim, no pressure if there's no damage.

File With Confidence

If there's damage, we document it thoroughly with photos and measurements so you can file a strong claim with your insurer.

Meet the Adjuster

We meet your insurance adjuster on-site to make sure nothing gets missed and your scope is written correctly.

Restore Your Home

Our crews complete the approved work to code and clean up fully. In most cases you pay only your deductible.
