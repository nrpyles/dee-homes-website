Smart Design, Bright Living

Reese Drive Two-Story proves that thoughtful design elevates any footprint. A contemporary shed-roof profile and clean two-tone exterior give way to an open-concept interior flooded with natural light — anchored by a quartz-topped island kitchen with stainless appliances.

Upstairs, the primary suite pairs an en-suite bath with a walk-in closet, joined by two secondary bedrooms — all seven minutes from Downtown Dallas. Sold in 2024.
