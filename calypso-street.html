The Project

Modern Luxury, Full Scale

Calypso Street Contemporary is Dee Homes at full expression: a grand double-height living space anchored by a floor-to-ceiling marble fireplace, a gourmet kitchen with quartz waterfall island and custom wood cabinetry, and clean stucco-and-cedar architecture with a cable-rail balcony.

Five bedrooms and five and a half baths across 3,356 square feet, with a covered patio, landscaped courtyard entry, and a spacious private backyard — minutes from Downtown Dallas, the Medical District, and Trinity Groves.

  • Double-height ceilings & grand staircase
  • Floor-to-ceiling marble linear fireplace
  • Quartz waterfall island & custom wood cabinets
  • Stucco & cedar exterior with cable-rail balcony
