Why Add On

Grow Without Leaving

Love your neighborhood but running out of room? An addition is often smarter than starting over — you keep your location, your lot, and your equity while gaining the space your family actually needs.

Dee Homes engineers every addition to blend seamlessly with your existing structure — matching rooflines, brick, siding, and trim so the new space looks original, not bolted on. One accountable builder handles design, permitting, structure, and every trade.

  • Primary suite & bedroom additions
  • Second-story pop-tops & expansions
  • Garage conversions, casitas & ADUs
  • Kitchen, living & sunroom expansions
