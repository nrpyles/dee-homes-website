The Dee Homes Difference

Craftsmen, Not Volume Builders

Founded by Dee Nsengiyumva and based in Frisco, Dee Homes LLC is backed by a team with more than a decade of construction experience, building and transforming the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex's most distinguished residences. Every project is tailored to the land it sits on and the family it serves.

Dee is personally involved in every build — clients work directly with the owner, not a rotating cast of project managers. It's the reason one client put it simply: "Dee was super nice, very efficient, knowledgeable, and got the job done quick!"

From ground-up custom homes to estate-level renovations, our design-build approach keeps one accountable team beside you from first sketch to final walkthrough — the reason our clients rate us a perfect 5.0 on HomeAdvisor and Angi.

  • True design-build: architecture, interiors & construction under one roof

  • Free, no-obligation estimates on every project
