Design-Build New Construction

One Team. Zero Guesswork.

Building new shouldn't mean juggling an architect, an engineer, and a builder who've never met. At Dee Homes, design and construction live under one roof — one contract, one schedule, one accountable team from lot to move-in.

Whether you own a lot in Frisco, acreage in Prosper, or an infill parcel near Downtown Dallas, we handle feasibility, design, permitting, and construction end to end — and we self-perform and coordinate every trade, from the foundation to the roof.

  • Lot & land feasibility before you commit
  • Architecture & interior selections in-house
  • Fixed, transparent line-item budgeting
  • Roofing, gutters, siding, electrical & HVAC coordinated in-house
