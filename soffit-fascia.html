Soffit & Fascia

Small Detail, Big Job

Soffit and fascia are the boards that close in your roofline — and they do more than look good. Fascia carries your gutters; soffit ventilates the attic while sealing out rain, rodents, and wasps. When they rot or get storm-damaged, the whole roof edge is at risk.

Dee Homes installs soffit and fascia on every new build and replaces damaged runs across the metroplex — in wood, composite, or aluminum, with proper attic ventilation built in.

  • New-construction & replacement soffit and fascia
  • Rot, water & storm damage repair
  • Proper attic ventilation & pest sealing
  • Wood, composite & aluminum — insurance claims welcome
