Modern Living, Minutes From Downtown

Spring Avenue Modern brought clean architectural lines and designer finish-out to one of Dallas's fastest-growing corridors. Each residence pairs an open-concept plan with vaulted ceilings, a quartz waterfall island, custom slat-wall fireplace, and white oak–toned flooring throughout.

Built for modern life: designer lighting packages, spa-inspired baths with honeycomb tile, private fenced yards with security gates, and a two-car garage for every home.
